Four decades of toolroom work behind every machine

Shivam Techno Industries is a manufacturer of Special Purpose Machines and custom automation, based in the Electronic Complex industrial area, Indore (M.P.).

Our founders have worked in precision toolrooms since 1982. That hands-on background is why our machines are designed around accuracy, easy maintenance and long service life, not just a good first demo.

  • Designed in-house. Our own engineers draw, review and build, so changes are quick.
  • Made for your part. Every machine is configured for your component and cycle time.

Where our machines fit

SPMs suit any plant that repeats the same operation in volume. Typical applications include:

  • Automotive components
  • Electrical and electronics
  • Pumps and valves
  • Plastics and moulding
  • Packaging
  • General engineering
  • Pharma and medical parts
  • Hardware and fasteners

Not on the list? If you repeat an operation on a part, we can likely automate it. Send us the drawing.

How a machine gets built

From first call to commissioning, one team handles it.

  1. Share your requirement

    Send the part drawing, material and target output. A call or WhatsApp message is enough to start.

  2. Design and quote

    Our engineers propose the machine layout, tooling and control, with a clear quote.

  3. Build and trial

    We fabricate and assemble in our Indore works, then run trials on your component.

  4. Delivery and support

    The machine is installed, your operators are shown how to run it, and we stay reachable after.

Questions we get often

Something else? Call or WhatsApp us and we will answer directly.

What do you need from me to give a quote?

Your part drawing or a sample, the material, the operation you want to automate and your target output per shift. If you only have an idea, call us and we will help you define it.

Can you build a machine for a part we already make?

Yes. Every machine is configured around your component, cycle time and floor space, so we start from your part and work backwards.

How long does a machine take to build?

It depends on the complexity of the machine. We confirm the schedule in the quote, before you commit.

Do you repair or upgrade existing machines?

Yes. We take up retrofitting, upgrades, repair and spares for special purpose machines and automation equipment.
